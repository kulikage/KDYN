The company

Energy R&D, backed by production engineering.

Kulicage Dynamics LLC is an R&D company in Charlotte, North Carolina, founded in 2024. Its core program, TorrideX, investigates plasma-assisted thermionic conversion of heat into electricity for compact, long-duration power.

The company is founder-led and builds on more than a decade of hands-on mechanical, hydraulic and production engineering, including taking a product from design to series production and sales.

R&D program

TorrideX: plasma-assisted thermionic conversion

How it works

A heated emitter releases electrons that cross a narrow gap to a cooler collector, producing DC power directly from heat. A low-pressure plasma in the gap supports electron transport by neutralizing space charge, the effect that otherwise limits current.

Where we are

Modeling and numerical verification: thermal, electrostatic and particle-transport models, tested for physical applicability before they are used to size hardware.

Next milestone

A controlled, electrically heated, non-radioactive bench experiment.

How we verify our models

  • Numerical results compared with analytical reference solutions
  • CPU and GPU implementations cross-checked, including an NVIDIA Warp benchmark
  • Grid-convergence and conservation-balance checks
  • Reproducibility of selected runs
  • Explicit tests of where model assumptions stop holding
